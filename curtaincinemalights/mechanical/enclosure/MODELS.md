## Component Fit Notes

- **D1 Mini** (34.2 × 25.6 × 10 mm): friction-fit in front pocket; USB-C port faces front wall cutout.
  Holds in place once lid is snapped. No mounting screws needed (no holes on D1 Mini PCB).
- **HLK-20M05** (56 × 32 × 22.5 mm): friction-fit in rear pocket; DIP AC pins face back wall, DC output
  faces left wall toward JST connector. The 22.5 mm module height fits within the 25 mm base wall.
- **74AHCT125 DIP-14**: sits on a small piece of stripboard between D1 Mini and PSU; held with double-sided foam tape.
  Alternatively, the level shifter can be soldered directly in-line without a PCB.
